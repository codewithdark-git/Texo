\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Introduce the topic for a reader who knows the general field but not your
corner of it. Explain the problem, why it is worth solving and what was
missing before this thesis.

\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:questions}

State the questions the thesis answers. Numbered questions are easy to
refer back to in the conclusion:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item What is the first question?
  \item What is the second question?
  \item How well does the proposed method answer them?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Contributions}
\label{sec:contributions}

List what is new in this work, and say which chapter presents each part.

\section{Structure of the Thesis}
\label{sec:structure}

Chapter~\ref{ch:background} reviews earlier work, Chapter~\ref{ch:method}
describes the method, Chapter~\ref{ch:results} presents the results, and
Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusion} answers the questions of
Section~\ref{sec:questions}. Appendix~\ref{app:a} holds supporting
material.

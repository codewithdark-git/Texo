\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} Conformal prediction gives distribution-free coverage, yet on
9{,}577 scans from three sites the per-group coverage drops to 81\%~for the
smallest source.\, We propose \textcolor{red}{groupwise calibration} and
{\color{blue} reweighting}, which restore nominal coverage\enspace(90\%) with
\textsc{Gcc}.\\
